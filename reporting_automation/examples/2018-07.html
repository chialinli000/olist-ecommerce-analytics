Reporting Periods and Interpretation Limits

  • Overall orders cover the full Orders dataset. Monthly operations cover January 2017 to August 2018. Orders are grouped by purchase month; statuses are those recorded in the dataset, not historical month-end status snapshots.
  • Fixed customer snapshot: 1 September 2018. Eligible orders were purchased before this date, have delivered status, and have matching customer and payment records. Customers are identified by customer_unique_id. This snapshot does not change with the selected operating month and may include later transactions.
  • Payment methods cover the full Payments dataset, without filtering by order status or month. Recorded payment value is not recognised revenue or profit. Order counts may overlap across payment methods.
  • Repeat-purchase rate is the proportion of customers with at least two eligible orders during the observation period, not cohort retention. RFM segments use predefined rules; future value and causal relationships have not been validated.
